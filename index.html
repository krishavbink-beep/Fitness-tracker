📚 Exercise guide

Bent-knee raises
Lie on your back, bend knees, gently bring them toward your chest.
Wall push-ups
Hands on wall, body straight; bend elbows and press away.
Dead bugs
On your back, move opposite arm and leg slowly while keeping your back comfortable.
Bird dogs
On hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg without twisting.
Reverse lunges
Step one foot back, bend both knees gently, then return upright.
